Thank you for taking the time to visit my travel guide!

This website reflects my experiences living in St. Petersburg during the summer of 2019. Since then, the political and travel landscape has changed significantly. While current travel may not be practical or fathomable for many visitors, I hope this site serves as a snapshot of the city's history, architecture, culture, and the experiences I had while living there.
